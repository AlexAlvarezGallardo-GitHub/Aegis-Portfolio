Every significant technical decision in Aegis is recorded as an **Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** following the standard template: **Context → Decision → Consequences**, plus alternatives considered.

## Current records

| ADR | Decision |
|-----|----------|
| ADR-001 | Fraud rules configuration — rules as data, versioned and hot-reloadable |
| ADR-002 | Kafka topic configuration — naming, partitions and retention per topic type |
| ADR-003 | Defer Kubernetes / Helm — local-first delivery until platform maturity |

## Why ADRs matter

ADRs capture the **reasoning** behind a decision, not just the outcome. Six months later, anyone on the team can answer *why* something was done a particular way — without re-litigating the decision.

Each record includes:

- **Context** — the forces at play when the decision was made.
- **Decision** — what was chosen, precisely.
- **Consequences** — the trade-offs accepted, both positive and negative.
- **Alternatives** — what else was considered and why it was rejected.

The list grows with the platform. New records are added as the architecture evolves.
